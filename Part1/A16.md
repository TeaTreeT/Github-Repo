A16. Discover 3 local security incidents.

1. UWA Data Breach - August 9–10, 2025
University data such as names, student IDs and photos, home addresses, phone numbers, emergency contact details, dates of birth, course details and unit grades, email addresses, country of birth, and citizenship status were stolen.
The university took preventative security measures by notifying staff and students and organising a uni-wide password reset.
There was no communication from the attackers at the time and no indication that it was ransomware.

2. University of Notre Dame Ransomware attack - Late January 2025
A group named Fog exfiltrated 62.2GB of data from the university such as contact details of employees and students, student medical documents, and confidential documents such as non-disclosure agreements.
This caused an operational impact for the university as their enrollments, timetables, academic transcripts, payroll and wifi were having issues.

3. Harry Perkins Institute of Medical Research Breach - July 2025
A ransomware gang named Medusa stole 4TBs of data from the hospital and were requesting $500,000 within a deadline.
After the deadline expired the data was leaked on the dark web. This included security camera footage, internal documents and non-sensitive research communications.
