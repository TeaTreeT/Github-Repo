A12. Discover 5 unique offline security tools.

1. Physical Lockbox
A mechanical box used to secure physical keys, cash, or documents.

2. USB Data Encryption Device
Encrypts files directly on the USB drive without needing internet access. Protects sensitive information even if the drive is lost or stolen.

3. Safe
A mechanical or electronic safe to secure physical keys, cash, or documents. No network or software is required, fully offline security
![IMG_2804](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2f50b222-19ce-494e-b383-29aaf2db94d4)

4. Document Shredder
Destroys sensitive documents offline to prevent unauthorized access.
![IMG_0905](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/308b4056-e5f7-4241-8eed-866b814c310e)

5. Hardware Firewall Appliance
Monitors and filters network traffic without cloud dependency. Common in offices or home labs where offline network segmentation is needed.
