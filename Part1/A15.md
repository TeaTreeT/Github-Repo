A15. Discover 5 recent security incidents.

1. Smile Team Orthodontics Data Breach – 10th March 2026
A ransomware group named SafePay listed stolen documents from the dental clinic on a darknet leak site. This breach included sensitive information such as staff details, home addresses, emails, medical certificates and medical history. Smile Team engaged external incident response managers and cybersecurity experts to urgently investigate and determine the scale of the impact.

2. Victorian Department of Education Data Breach – January 2026
Hackers gained unauthorised access to the Victorian Department of Education’s systems, exposing personal data including names, school details, email addresses and encrypted passwords of students across government schools. The department forced password resets and worked with security teams to contain the breach and safeguard accounts.

3. Hasbro Systems Taken Offline – 28th March 2026
US toy manufacturer Hasbro detected unauthorised access to its network on 28th March and took certain systems offline as a precaution. The company activated its incident response protocols and engaged third‑party cybersecurity experts to investigate the breach. Recovery work and investigation into the scope and potential data impact are ongoing.

4. Hazeldenes Cyberattack – February 2026
Australian poultry processor Hazeldenes suffered a cyberattack that disrupted production at its processing plant, leading to shortages for pubs and retailers. Hazeldenes worked with cybersecurity investigators to identify the cause and restore systems, prioritising operational continuity while assessing the incident’s impact.

5. Regis Resources Cyber Incident – January 2026
ASX‑listed gold producer Regis Resources confirmed a cyber incident after its network was reportedly targeted by hackers, with the Lynx ransomware group claiming responsibility. The company stated its security measures contained the incident, preventing operational disruption or confirmed data theft, and continues investigations to determine impact.
