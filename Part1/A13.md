A13. Discover 5 unique online security tools.

1. Multi Factor Authentication Apps
Adds an extra verification step for online accounts by adding an extra step to authenticate a user. eg. Google Authenticator which generates codes and push notifications

2. Virtual Private Networks
Encrypts internet traffic to protect privacy and prevent eavesdropping. eg. NordVPN

3. Online Password Managers
Stores and encrypts passwords online and makes them accessible from multiple devices. Eg. LastPass

4. Cloud Based Anti Virus / Malware Apps
Protects computers and networks by scanning threats via the cloud. eg. Norton 360

5. Web Application Firewalls
Protects web applications from online attacks like SQL injection or DDos. eg. Cloudfare
