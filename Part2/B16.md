B16. Survey the current state-of-the-art solutions in cybersecurity.

Cybersecurity is now heavily influenced by AI-powered and integrated security systems. Platforms like Extended Detection and Response (XDR),  pull together data from endpoints, networks, and cloud environments to detect and defend threats far more efficiently than older tools. One of the standout strategies today is Zero Trust Architecture, which operates on the principle that no user or device can be automatically trusted. It demands ongoing verification of identity and access rights. Moreover, artificial intelligence and machine learning are playing a greater role in malware detection, enabling systems to recognise new threats by analysing behavioural patterns rather than relying solely on known signatures. Cloud-native security platforms also step up by offering protection for distributed systems, they keep an eye on configurations, workloads, and APIs. Modern intrusion detection systems like Suricata work hand-in-hand with SIEM tools and automation to deliver quick monitoring and response capabilities. All these technologies show a move towards a more proactive, intelligent, and integrated approach to cybersecurity defence.
