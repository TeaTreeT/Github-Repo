B10. Participate in a cybersecurity activity as part of job/internship/volunteering etc.

At my workplace at a small pharmacy one of my roles is to monitor the security cameras and check for security threats, intrusion, or suspicious behaviour.

For example, in April while at work one of the staff mentioned that someone was acting suspicious earlier while in shop. 
So I checked the security cameras and got footage of the customer going behind the counter and stealing prohibited medication.
This incident was then promptly reported to the correct authorities.
